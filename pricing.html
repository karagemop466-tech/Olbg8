Pricing every sport

How this desk turns a verified input into a line. The method is fixed per sport so the same inputs always produce the same price, and every published line can be re-derived by hand.

The arithmetic

  1. Estimate a probability for the selection from the sport's verified inputs (form table, head-to-head record, ranking points, recent scoring rates, field size for races).
  2. Add the desk margin to that probability, because these are originated lines, not market offers. Margins are published per sport below.
  3. Convert the adjusted probability to decimal then American. Decimal above 2.00 becomes positive American, below 2.00 becomes negative.
  4. Clamp to the mandate window (−250 to +10000 American). A selection whose honest price is shorter than −250 is published as a pick but flagged as outside the window.
  5. Band the confidence by the implied probability of the published line: high ≥ 65%, medium 55–64.9%, low < 55%.

Standing guards

  • No line is published without an official source for the event's identity and for the form inputs used in the calculation.
  • Where a required input (starting pitcher, lineup, going, weather, injury) is unconfirmed, the line is withheld and the match is flagged rather than priced on an assumption.
  • Margins are stated per market so a reader can back out the desk's raw probability: raw = published probability / (1 + margin).
  • The mandate window is -250 to +10000 American. A line shorter than -250 is still published where the analysis supports it, but is labelled out-of-window.
  • Confidence bands follow the published implied probability: high 65.0-71.4% (which is -186 to -250 American), medium 55.0-64.9%, low below 55.0%.
  • Every price is originated by this desk. No bookmaker price is copied, and no OLBG tip count is treated as a probability.
