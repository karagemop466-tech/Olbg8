How we originate a line when nobody gives us one

OLBG does not print a bookmaker price in the markup a reader can see. What it prints, for every selection, is how many tipsters picked it and how many tips the whole market carries — and OLBG's own definition of its Value Rating says it treats that share as “the theoretical probability of winning”. We use the same quantity as our input, price it ourselves, and publish the arithmetic.

1 · Read the market

For each event, every market OLBG exposes: win match or money line, handicap or spread, totals, and the player markets such as man of the match and top batsman.

2 · Count, don't guess

Take the printed “x / y” — tips on this selection over tips on the market. No bookmaker number is copied and no number is interpolated.

3 · Fix the outcome count

k = 2 for a head-to-head, 3 where a draw is priced (OLBG's handball money line), and the declared field size for an outright — 78 at the Maybank, 128 at the World Darts Championship. No published field size means no price above a floor, and a flag.

4 · Shrink toward the base rate

p = (n + 8/k) ÷ (T + 8). A Dirichlet update with a uniform prior, so one lonely tip on a 78-player field cannot read as 50%, and 1 of 1 on a match cannot read as certain.

5 · Price it

Fair decimal = 1/p, converted to American. Our offered line takes a 4% margin, and the no-margin figure is printed next to it so anyone can reverse the step.

6 · Apply the band

Lines shorter than -250 are rejected from the picks surface and flagged. That removes exactly the “sure thing” favourites the brief ruled out; a +10,000 ceiling stops a one-tip market becoming a lottery ticket.

Confidence bands, as actually applied

BandRequirement, in full
HighOfficial governing-body, league-API or club source opened and matched to the fixture, and priced chance ≥ 60%, and at least 4 tips in that market, and inside the band. Outrights cannot reach High at all.
MediumEither a corroborated fixture at ≥ 52%, or an uncorroborated fixture at ≥ 60% carrying 8+ tips.
LowEverything else — including anything priced from fewer than three tips, which is capped here whatever else it has.

Applied today: official crosswalk AND p>=0.6 AND market tips>=4 AND in window → 1 rows. 46 rows carry fewer than three tips and are capped to Low on purpose; that is the honest answer to a request for high-confidence picks on markets that are mostly empty.
