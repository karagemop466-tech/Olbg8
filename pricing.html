How we price every match

We do not copy a bookmaker. We build a number from official results and then convert that number into a price. The method is the same for every sport; only the parameters change.

1. Rating input

For each competitor we take the official season record: matches played, and points or goals or runs for and against. Nothing else. Where a competition has a table, the table is the input. Where it does not, we do not price the match.

2. Shrink the sample

A seven-game point differential is noisy, so every rating is pulled toward the league average before use:

rating = (points_for - points_against) / games_played × games_played / (games_played + K)

With K around 8 to 12 for a short European season and 60 for a 162-game baseball season, a club that has played a handful of matches cannot produce an extreme number.

3. Turn the ratings into a projected margin

projected margin = home rating - away rating + home edge, where the home edge is the sport-specific bump in the table below. The projected margin is also our handicap number, rounded to the sport's scoring step.

4. Turn the margin into a probability

We treat the actual margin as normally distributed around the projection with the sport's own standard deviation. That gives the chance of the favourite covering any line, and for handball and rugby union it also gives the chance of a draw, so those two sports are priced as three-way markets.

5. Convert to a price

price = -100 × p / (1 - p) when p is at least 50%, otherwise 100 × (1 - p) / p. These are fair prices with no margin. A bookmaker's price is shorter; that gap is where a bet becomes interesting.

Worked example: Dax v Provence

Provence have played 8 matches with 276 points scored and 161 conceded, so their raw differential is plus 14.4. Shrunk with K = 6 it becomes plus 8.2. Dax are minus 4.6 raw, minus 2.6 shrunk. Adding the 3.5 home edge for Dax gives a projected margin of minus 7.4 in Provence's favour, which we round to a 7.5-point handicap. The chance of Provence winning outright comes out at 70.8%, which converts to -243. That is the one High-confidence pick of the day that also sits inside the -250 to +10000 window.

Markets we publish for every priced match

  • Match winner — the price on each side, plus the draw where the sport allows one.
  • Handicap — the projected margin, quoted as a line, with a price on each side.
  • Total — the combined score the two records imply, rounded to the sport's step.

Markets we do not price: player props, correct score, and anything that needs team news. No injury, suspension or lineup information was read for this edition, so no market depends on it.
