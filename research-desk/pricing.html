Publication and calculation policy

Proposed confidence labels: high ≥70%, medium 55–70%, low <55%, only after calibration. Probability is not evidence quality. High-confidence publication also requires positive expected value, official match evidence and uncertainty review.

Allowed offered odds: −250 through +10000, inclusive. At −250 the break-even probability is 71.43%; at +10000 it is 0.99%. A 70% estimate is not a value bet at −250. No odds imply certainty.

Fair decimal odds = 1 / p for no-push outcomes. With pushes, fair decimal = 1 + P(loss) / P(win). EV per unit = P(win) × (decimal odds − 1) − P(loss). Keep draw, push, dead-heat, retirement, withdrawal and overtime rules separate.

Proposed workflow: official fixture identity → current roster and form → independently fitted distribution → all observed markets → quoted price and timestamp → value and uncertainty gates → original 30+ word analysis → stale/start-time checks. Unknown facts remain blank. No unsupported “no injuries” claims.

Implemented: tested probability/price arithmetic and conservative publication guard in scripts/pricing.py. Not implemented: fitted sport-specific engines, calibrated probabilities, exhaustive market feeds or automated editorial production.
