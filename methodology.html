Methodology

Line origination, confidence bands, publication gate and source policy

How lines are originated

Each pick starts from an implied probability p for the selection. American price: if p ≥ 0.50, price = −100 × p/(1−p); if p < 0.50, price = 100 × (1−p)/p. Worked example: p = 0.70 → −100 × 0.70/0.30 = −233. p = 0.55 → −100 × 0.55/0.45 = −122.

Odds window: all published lines sit between −250 and +10,000 (American). Fair prices shorter than −250 (e.g. −355, −400) are held at the −250 boundary and flagged with a line note; the implied probability shown is at the displayed price.

Confidence bands: High = implied probability ≥ 70%; Medium = 60–69%; Low = below 60%. Confidence reflects the strength of official-source confirmation, not just price.

Markets investigated

For every event this desk looked at win-match/moneyline, handicap (puck line / point spread / games handicap) and total markets where OLBG or the official source exposed them. Published selections are moneyline/win-match or handicap picks as stated on each card; totals are not published because OLBG did not expose total lines with tipster consensus at capture.

Publication gate

  • A prediction is published only when it is fully written (unique prose, ≥30 words), has a selection, a line inside the window, at least one official source link, and a confidence band.
  • The words “model” and “verified” never appear in published predictions (they are internal-use only).
  • If a value cannot be confirmed from an official source, it is left blank and the row is flagged. Nothing is inferred or filled in.
  • Drafts that do not clear the gate are withheld, not published.

Source policy

  • OLBG.com supplies event discovery and tipster consensus only; it is never treated as verification of form, stats or injuries.
  • Official fixtures: NHL schedule/game centre, MLB StatsAPI, EuroLeague site, ICC fixtures, LNR Pro D2 calendar, EHF Champions League calendar, ATP Tour, GAA fixtures, TVF/legavolley/CEV references.
  • Blank fields stay blank; times are UTC and may differ from OLBG’s local-time labels — discrepancies are flagged.

Limitations

  • Six of the 15 requested sports (Golf, Darts, Motor Racing, Rugby League, Snooker, Greyhounds) had no actionable match-level OLBG listings at capture; no picks are fabricated for them.
  • Hurling is not among the requested sports and is not covered.
  • Some ATP, CEV, URC and EuroLeague sources return no current match data or 404; affected rows are marked OLBG-only.
  • All times are capture-time (27 Oct 2026, UTC); recheck tipoffs and lineups before betting.
