Pricing method

One arithmetic pipeline creates every line on this site. It is deliberately simple, deterministic and reproducible from scripts/pricing.py — no black box, no discretionary tweaks per match.

1. Market probability (de-vig)

For each market we read every displayed decimal price. The raw implied probability of an outcome is 1 / decimal. The overround is the sum of those values. Our market probability is p_market = (1 / decimal) / overround, so the bookmaker's margin is removed proportionally across the outcome set. Markets whose overround falls below 0.90 (an incomplete outcome set) are not priced at all.

2. Tipster consensus

OLBG publishes the number of win tips on each outcome. With N tips in the market and t on an outcome, we shrink the raw share toward the market probability: p_tip = (t + 6·p_market) / (N + 6). Six pseudo-tips is enough to stop a one-tip market from moving a price materially, while still letting a 20-tip consensus register.

3. Our fair probability

p_fair = (1 − w)·p_market + w·p_tip with w = min(0.35, N / (N + 12)). With no tips w is zero and the line is the pure de-vigged market. With 20 tips w reaches the 0.35 cap. This is why a pick can differ from the displayed price without ever ignoring it.

4. Our prices

Fair American price: for p ≥ 0.5, −100p/(1−p); otherwise +100(1−p)/p. Our offered price is the fair decimal price reduced by a 3% margin. The offered price is what we would need to be paid; it is not a market quote.

5. Confidence tiers

High = our probability ≥ 70% · Medium = 55–70% · Low = below 55%. The requested price band is −250 to +10,000 on the displayed price. Note the arithmetic consequence: −250 implies 71.4%, so a high-confidence pick inside the band can only exist between 70.0% and 71.4%. That is why the board carries one high-confidence pick and a large medium tier, rather than labelling 60% favourites as high confidence.

6. Selection rules

  • Only selections inside the −250 to +10,000 band are eligible.
  • Match markets need a fair probability of at least 45%; outright markets (tournament, race, championship) at least 8%.
  • One pick per event, except golf, darts and motor racing outrights where two selections per event are allowed.
  • OLBG's "Gaelic Sports" category is published as a single sport (Gaelic football and hurling together).
  • Ranking is by confidence tier, then fair probability, then value at the displayed price.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not invent a fixture, participant, injury, statistic or lineup. If a field is blank, it was not confirmed.
  • We do not price a market with an incomplete outcome set.
  • We do not treat a draw-heavy tipster consensus as a draw probability: any draw selection is capped at three percentage points from its market probability.
  • We do not claim our offered price is available anywhere; the displayed OLBG price is the reference for value.
