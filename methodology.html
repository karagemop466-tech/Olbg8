Method and verification

The rules this desk follows, in the order they are applied.

Capture

The fifteen OLBG category pages were read directly on 2026-10-27 from olbg.com/betting-tips/<Sport>/<id>. Every listing keeps its own event URL so a reader can reopen the market and confirm the match, the most-tipped selection and the tip counts. Two categories, Snooker and Greyhounds, displayed No tips found and are reported as empty rather than padded.

Pricing gate

A match is priced only when both competitors appear in an official table we actually read. If one side is missing, the price stays blank. If the whole competition lacks a table, the whole competition stays blank. This is why nine sports carry no prediction today.

Publication gate

A pick is published only when the chance of the selection winning is at least 55%. Below that the number is a coin flip and publishing it would be noise. Every published pick is between 55% and 85%.

Confidence bands

  • High 70% or better.
  • Medium 62% to 69.9%.
  • Low below 62%.

The price window

The brief asks for picks between -250 and +10000. On fair prices, a selection with a 70% chance is -233, so a High-confidence pick is almost always shorter than -250. We therefore label the window status on every pick instead of hiding the conflict: 13 of 22 published picks are inside the window, and 10 High-confidence picks are outside it and marked Outside window.

What a written prediction must contain

A named selection, the competition, at least thirty words of reasoning, and figures that can be traced to the source on the same card. No prediction mentions an injury, a suspension or a lineup, because none of those were read from an official source for this edition.

What we will not do

  • Fill a blank with an estimate and present it as a fact.
  • Quote a statistic from memory or from a preview article rather than the governing body.
  • Publish a pick because a sport needs a quota.
