Pricing playbook

One audit trail for every line.

Sport-specific inputs differ; publication controls do not.

Universal release pipeline

1. Discover an OLBG event → 2. Confirm it on the organizer, league or governing-body site → 3. Record timestamped form, availability and event conditions → 4. Price every supported outcome → 5. Remove margin from comparison odds → 6. Stress-test uncertainty → 7. Publish only eligible high-confidence edges.

Confidence bands

High: ≥70% fair probability and evidence complete. Medium: 55–69.9%. Low: <55%. Confidence is probability, not certainty. Missing official evidence overrides the band and blocks publication.

American fair odds: for probability p ≥ 0.5, −100p/(1−p); otherwise +100(1−p)/p. Preserve full precision internally and round only for display.

Sport-by-sport methods

Golf

Outright, each-way, first-round leader, matchups

Start with field-strength adjusted strokes-gained projections by surface/course fit; simulate round scores with weather and tee-time effects; normalize outright probabilities to 100% and derive matchup prices from paired simulations.

Volleyball

Match winner, set handicap, total points, correct score

Estimate side-out and break-point rates by rotation from opponent-adjusted recent sets; simulate best-of-five scoring with venue and confirmed lineup adjustments.

Rugby Union

Match winner, handicap, total points

Use opponent-adjusted expected tries and penalty points, home advantage, rest and confirmed XV strength; fit correlated scoring distributions for moneyline, spread and total.

Cricket

Match winner, innings total, player performance, outright

Project runs and wickets by format from venue, toss, weather and confirmed XI; simulate innings ball-by-ball with batter/bowler matchup rates and chase-state effects.

Snooker

Match winner, frame handicap, correct score, outright

Convert opponent-adjusted frame win rates from scoring, break-building and safety indicators; apply a best-of-N binomial structure with deciding-frame sensitivity.

Darts

Match winner, handicap, total legs/sets, outright

Estimate leg hold and break probabilities from three-dart average, first-nine average, doubles and throw order; simulate the exact event format.

Baseball

Moneyline, run line, game total

Build run distributions from confirmed starters, bullpen availability, batting quality, park and weather; use correlated team Poisson/negative-binomial simulations.

Gaelic Sports

Gaelic Football and Hurling: match winner, handicap, total points

Rate goal and point production separately using opponent-adjusted form, venue and confirmed teams; simulate scoring under competition-specific rules.

Greyhounds

Race winner, place, forecast

Project sectional and finishing times from graded runs, trap, track/distance, going and crowding risk; simulate each runner jointly and normalize win probabilities across the field.

Tennis

Match winner, set/game handicap, total games, correct score

Estimate surface-adjusted serve and return point probabilities with recency and fitness; simulate points, games and sets under the tournament format.

Motor Racing

Race winner, podium, matchup, qualifying

Combine practice/qualifying pace, degradation, grid position, reliability, strategy and weather; run lap-level simulations with safety-car and retirement states.

Handball

Moneyline, handicap, total goals

Estimate pace and attack/defence efficiency from opponent-adjusted possessions, goalkeeper rates, venue and lineups; simulate correlated goal totals.

Ice Hockey

Moneyline, puck line, game total

Use expected goals, shot quality, special teams, rest and confirmed goaltenders; simulate regulation and overtime separately.

Basketball

Moneyline, point spread, game total

Project possessions and lineup-adjusted offensive/defensive efficiency with rest and travel; simulate correlated scores and convert margins to moneyline/spread/total probabilities.

Rugby League

Match winner, handicap, total points

Estimate set efficiency, field position, tries and goal-kicking from opponent-adjusted form, venue and confirmed squads; simulate correlated scores.
