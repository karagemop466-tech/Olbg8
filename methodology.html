Methodology

How the OLBG feed was reverse-engineered, how lines are originated, and what was checked.

Every step is reproducible from the committed snapshots and scripts. No fixture, price, statistic, injury or prediction is invented.

1 · Reverse engineering OLBG

OLBG's sport hubs are SvelteKit pages. Appending /__data.json to a hub URL (for example the Tennis hub) returns the flattened server payload. The same endpoint exists on each event page and exposes every market, selection, decimal/American/fractional price, tip count, confidence value, event schema and start time. The collector fetches 16 hubs, 16 “All Events” pages and 201 individual event payloads, then decodes the flattened devalue arrays without loss.

2 · Originating a price for every market

For every market: p_i = 1/decimal_i; q_i = p_i / sum(p) removes the displayed overround; c_i = win_tips_i / sum(win_tips) is the OLBG consensus; w = 0.28 * total_tips/(total_tips+8); fair_i ∝ (1-w)q_i + w*c_i (+ a small official-strength tilt on two-sided result markets). fair_i converts to an American price.

The blend is deliberately conservative. A market with no tips produces a pure de-vigged price; a market with many tips moves toward consensus but never more than 28% of the weight. On two-sided result markets where an official standings feed covers both teams, a small documented strength tilt is added. The result is normalised back to 100% and converted to an American price. Where only one side of a market has a price, or the displayed overround is unusually high, that is flagged on the row.

3 · Confidence and the price band

High: edge >= 1.5 pp, >= 3 market tips, >= 55% consensus, and an official event/competition link, official form/statistic, or selection-specific official ranking. Medium: edge >= 0.8 pp with >= 2 tips or official support. Low: otherwise. All published picks must display between -250 and +10000. All high-confidence picks are therefore in the requested −250 to +10000 band rather than being short-priced favourites above 70% implied probability.

4 · Official evidence

Official NHL, MLB, WTA, EHF, Formula 1, ICC, Cricket Australia, CEV, EPCR, LNR, GAA, GBGB, PDC, WST, PGA TOUR, LPGA, NBA, NRL and Super League endpoints were captured at the same time. Team/player names were normalised and matched against those snapshots. Event-specific game-centre links were created for NHL and MLB fixtures; other sports use competition-level official pages. Where a match was not present in an official capture, the official link is a directory and the row is flagged. Injury and lineup sheets were not published in these captures, so those fields are blank.

5 · Verification performed

  • Re-decoded every captured OLBG payload and confirmed each event has a source URL and a start time.
  • Checked that every generated table row carries an official-source link and an OLBG link.
  • Checked every written prediction for uniqueness, length (≥30 words), a named selection, a confidence label, an in-band price, and the absence of internal jargon.
  • Rebuilt the site from scratch and checked every internal link resolves.
  • Compared official NHL/MLB/NBA game times with OLBG times and flagged the differences.

7 · Limitations

OLBG publishes tipped events, not a complete fixture database. Displayed prices are OLBG quotes at snapshot time, not live bookmaker offers. Official injury sheets were not published in the captured sources; those fields stay blank and are flagged. Our fair price is a transparent research line, not a guarantee or a prediction of outcome certainty.
