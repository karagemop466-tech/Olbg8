How this sport is priced

Two-way match market. Player strength comes from tour ranking points and recent win rate on the same surface, blended into an Elo-style rating; the rating gap becomes a match probability, then a per-set probability for set and total-games markets.

Worked example: A 120-point rating edge converts to 68.5% in a best-of-three; with the 3.5% margin the line is -231 and the published win chance is 69.9%.
