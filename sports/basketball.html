How this sport is priced

Two-way market including overtime. Team strength is a points-per-100-possession differential adjusted for schedule and opponent, converted to a projected margin, then to a win probability with a margin standard deviation of 11.5 points.

Worked example: A projected 6.4-point home margin gives Phi(6.4/11.5) = 71.1% home; the 4% margin pushes the published line to about -285 and the pick is flagged out-of-window.
