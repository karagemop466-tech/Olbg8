The Antonelli-excluded championship market is where the real price sits

Formula 1 Drivers Championship 2026 Without Kimi Antonelli · OLBG prints “06 Dec 08:00”

Low

Bet: Max Verstappen — Win Tournament · our line -114 · chance 53.3% · OLBG support 4/7

OLBG lists a version of the drivers' title market with Kimi Antonelli removed, and four of the seven tips on it name Max Verstappen, which our origination prices at -114. The structure of that market is what makes it interesting rather than the tipping: Antonelli needs only a top-nine finish in Mexico City to be champion whatever anyone else does, so on the day this residual market almost certainly resolves against anyone still in it, and the price of -114 for the best remaining name is not a reflection of the standalone chance. Do not back Verstappen here; if you already hold the position, note that the exclusion of one driver is doing all the work.
