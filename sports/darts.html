How this sport is priced

Two-way match market in legs or sets. Strength is three-dart average and checkout percentage; legs are converted to a match probability through a per-leg binomial or a set-based equivalent.

Worked example: A 0.60 per-leg edge in a best-of-19 legs match gives 71.3%, which sits at the very edge of the price window.
