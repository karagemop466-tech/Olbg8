How this sport is priced

Two-way moneyline and run line. Strength is a run-expectation model driven by the confirmed starting pitchers, the bullpen and the park; the win probability comes from a run-scoring simulation with a 4.3-run standard deviation.

Worked example: Expected runs 4.6 to 4.0 gives a 57.5% home win with the stated spread; the line lands near -135.
