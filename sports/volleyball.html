How this sport is priced

Two-way match market with no draw. Each side is rated from sets and points won per set across the current league season and from head-to-head set differential; the rating gap is converted to a match probability with the standard logistic link, then adjusted for home court (worth 0.15 to 0.30 of a set) and, for set-score markets, expanded through a per-set binomial.

Worked example: A side rated 0.62 per set in a best-of-five wins 3-0 at 23.8%, 3-1 at 26.6% and 3-2 at 24.7%, so the match probability is 75.1%; after the 4% margin the published line is about -270, which is inside the window only if the band check passes.
