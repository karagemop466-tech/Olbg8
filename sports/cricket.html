How this sport is priced

Two-way match market and, in Test cricket, a three-way market with the draw. T20 and ODI probabilities come from a scoring-rate model; Test probabilities are driven by first-innings run expectation and remaining time.

Worked example: A projected 18-run edge in a T20 gives 66.9% for the stronger side before the toss adjustment.
