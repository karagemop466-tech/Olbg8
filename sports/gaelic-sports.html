How this sport is priced

Two-way market in Gaelic football and hurling, plus handicap and total points. Strength is scoring difference per game adjusted for division and opponent; draws are possible but rare and are priced as a no-bet on the two-way market.

Worked example: A projected 5-point football margin gives 70.1% for the favourite, which is inside the window at about -215.
