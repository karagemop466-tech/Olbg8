How this sport is priced

Three-way regulation market and two-way market including overtime and shootout. Strength is goal differential per 60 minutes at even strength; regulation probabilities come from a Poisson goal model, and the draw is removed to price the two-way market.

Worked example: Expected goals 3.15 to 2.70 gives a 44.1% regulation home win, 24.6% draw and 31.3% away; splitting the draw 55/45 yields 57.6% home on the two-way market.
