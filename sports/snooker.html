How this sport is priced

Two-way match market in a best-of format. Strength is a per-frame probability built from ranking, recent frame differential and head-to-head; the match probability is the binomial sum over the required frames.

Worked example: A 0.58 per-frame edge in a best-of-nine gives 66.3% for the match, with the most likely scoreline 5-3.
