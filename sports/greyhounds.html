How this sport is priced

Race outright market across the six to eight runners, plus forecast and place markets. Strength is early pace and recent sectional times adjusted for trap and going; probabilities are normalised across the field.

Worked example: A runner at 26% raw becomes 22.7% after the 12% field margin, which is about +340 American.
