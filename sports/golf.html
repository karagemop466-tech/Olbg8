How this sport is priced

Tournament outright and place markets. Strength is strokes-gained per round over the last 24 rounds adjusted for course fit; win probability is normalised across the field and place probability uses the Harville approximation.

Worked example: A field-leading 8% raw win share becomes 6.9% after the field margin, which is about +1350 American.
