How this sport is priced

Three-way market (win, draw, loss) with handicap and total points. Strength is points differential adjusted for opponent quality; the draw is modelled explicitly at about 2% to 4% depending on the expected margin.

Worked example: A projected 7-point home margin puts the home win at 69.6% and the draw at 2.9%; the away win takes the remaining 27.5%.
