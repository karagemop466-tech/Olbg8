How this sport is priced

Markets covered: Moneyline, handicap, total goals.

Estimate pace and attack/defence efficiency from opponent-adjusted possessions, goalkeeper rates, venue and lineups; simulate correlated goal totals.

Universal method: proportional de-vig across the full outcome set, Bayesian tipster-consensus blend (weight capped at 0.35), 3% offered-price margin, and the −250 to +10,000 eligibility band. See pricing method.
