How this sport is priced

Three-way market in most European leagues and two-way in some cup formats. Strength is goals for and against per match; expected goals come from an attack-versus-defence product, and the win probabilities come from a Poisson grid.

Worked example: Expected goals 30.5 to 27.0 gives a 54.9% home win, 12.1% draw and 33.0% away win.
