Sports below the five-pick target
OLBG listed fewer supported markets than five picks in snooker (0), baseball (1 event), darts (2), motor racing (2), gaelic football/hurling (4), rugby league (3) and golf (3). No fixture was invented to fill the gap.

Form and injury feeds missing for most sports
Official injury/lineup feeds were captured only for the NHL and MLB (and the F1 classification). For tennis, cricket, rugby, handball, volleyball, basketball, darts, golf, gaelic games and greyhounds no official team-news source was retrievable in this snapshot, so those notes are blank and marked unconfirmed per row.
source
NBA, ATP, EHF, GBGB and URC endpoints blocked
Requests to cdn.nba.com (403), atptour.com (403), ehfcl.eurohandball.com (404), gbgb.org.uk (404) and unitedrugby.com (404) returned no usable data, so no official facts are claimed for those competitions.
source
Greyhound races have no tipster consensus and no date
Greyhound race pages embed the full field and prices but the listing shows a time only (for example 9:46 Nottingham). Our greyhound lines are single-market and the picks sit in the Low tier.
source
Draw-heavy three-way markets
OLBG tipsters pile onto the draw in handball and gaelic football (Tie was the most-tipped selection in ten handball matches). Our line caps any draw deviation from the market at three percentage points so a small tip sample cannot manufacture a draw probability.
source
Four markets withheld
Markets whose displayed prices summed to an overround below 0.90 (an incomplete outcome set) were not priced at all rather than priced on partial information.
source
Prices are a snapshot, not a live quote
Every price on this site is the decimal/ American/ fractional figure OLBG displayed at capture time. Our own offered price adds a 3% margin to the fair line and is not a market quote.
source
Long-dated outrights
Several picks settle in 2027 (PDC World Championship, All-Ireland championships, State of Origin, Super League, NRL). Squads and fields cannot be confirmed yet.
source
No bet placement or live odds re-check
The dashboard does not connect to a bookmaker. Prices must be re-checked on OLBG before any bet, and a moved price invalidates the stated edge.
