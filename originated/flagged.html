high  OLBG's handball tip share cannot be a probability distribution

OLBG returns 'Tie' as the most-tipped outcome on 14 of 15 handball listings, including every EHF Champions League match, with shares between 43% and 78%. Handball settles in regulation or by shoot-out; a draw is not a possible outcome of the market as bettors settle it. Either the label is a rendering artefact of a draw-no-bet style market or the tip data is corrupt. No handball line is originated from it, and all 15 events are left unpriced rather than priced on a broken anchor.
