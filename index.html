How to read a pick

  • Bet — the market and the side: match winner, handicap, total, set winner, race place, etc.
  • Our line — the American price this desk originates for that side, not a price quoted by any bookmaker.
  • Win chance — the probability behind the line, after the desk margin is removed from the raw estimate.
  • Sources — one link to the OLBG listing and at least one link to the official body used for the form facts.
