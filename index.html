How the lines are made

Every displayed price is de-vigged across the full outcome set to give a market probability, blended with the OLBG tipster consensus using a Bayesian shrinkage term, then republished as our fair American price and a 3% margin offered price. Confidence: High ≥ 70%, Medium 55–70%, Low < 55%. The exact formulas, thresholds and edge cases are on the pricing method page; the raw inputs are in data/lines.json and data/picks.json.

ℹ
What is confirmed and what is not. Fixture, market, price and tip counts are read directly from the linked OLBG event page. Recent form and injury data are only stated where an official feed (NHL, MLB, F1) supplied them; for the other sports those fields are left blank and the pick is marked unconfirmed in Flagged for review.
