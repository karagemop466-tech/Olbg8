What is deliberately missing

  • Handball — 15 events captured, 0 priced. OLBG returns “Tie” as the most-tipped outcome on 14 of 15 listings, including every EHF Champions League match. A draw cannot be the modal result of a sport that always settles, so the tip share is not treated as a probability.
  • Snooker and Greyhounds — 0 events. Both hubs return “No tips found.” with no live fixtures.
  • Numeric odds on OLBG win markets. They do not exist. OLBG renders tip counts and confidence percentages only, so the market column is blank rather than invented.
  • Totals and handicaps without a sourced expectation. Where OLBG quotes a line value but publishes no expected total, the over/under is left unpriced.

Prices are originated from OLBG tip shares shrunk toward explicitly stated priors, and from sourced market lines where they exist.
