What is verified and what is not

The distinction is important: a market price and a governing-body fact are different things.

1 · Market facts

Event names, start times, market names, selections, OLBG prices and tip counts come directly from the captured OLBG SvelteKit payloads. Every row links to its OLBG event page.

2 · Official facts

Form, standings, rankings, results and fixture identity come from official league/tour feeds captured at the same time. Where an official page was blocked, that is flagged rather than filled in.

3 · Our calculations

Fair prices, edges and confidence labels are our own transparent calculations, not bookmaker offers. Read the formula.
