Snooker Winning Margins and Handicap Betting Stats
The stats in this section and the two that follow cover the last ten completed editions of each Triple Crown event: the World Championship, the Masters and the UK Championship. The figures come from OLBG's own database of results and cover the main stages only, from the last 32 at the World Championship and UK Championship and from the last 16 at the Masters. Walkovers are excluded.
Match length shapes margins. Average winning margins range from 3.6 to 4.7 frames at the World Championship, where matches are longer, and from 2.5 to 3.1 frames in the best of 11 rounds at the Masters and UK Championship. A player on a -1.5 frame handicap needs to win by two or more frames, while a player on +1.5 covers by winning or losing by a single frame. A match that does not go to a deciding frame finishes by two or more frames, and 76.1% of Masters and UK Championship matches from the last 16 to the semi-finals finished that way.
