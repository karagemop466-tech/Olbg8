Volleyball Scoring Explained
Understanding how volleyball is scored makes it easier to follow the volleyball tips and predictions above, particularly for the Set Score market.
How a volleyball match is won
Volleyball uses rally scoring, so a point is awarded at the end of every rally, whichever team served. A team wins a set by reaching the target score with a lead of at least two points, so a close set can run beyond the target. Most senior matches are played as best of five sets, and the first team to win three sets wins the match.

What this means for volleyball predictions
There are no draws in volleyball, so every match has a winner and Win Match tips are always settled. Because the match ends as soon as one team wins three sets, it can finish in three, four or five sets. Those finishing scores are the outcomes our tipsters choose between in Set Score tips, which are explained in the markets section below.
Volleyball Betting Markets: Win Match and Set Score
OLBG's volleyball tips cover two markets: Win Match and Set Score.
Win Match tips
There are no draws in volleyball, so every match produces a winner. For Win Match volleyball predictions, our tipsters pick either the home or the away team to win.
Set Score tips
For Set Score tips, our tipsters predict the exact score in sets. Matches are best of five (see
how volleyball scoring works
), so there are six possible outcomes, shown here as home sets to away sets:
