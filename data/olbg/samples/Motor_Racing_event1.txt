2026 Mexican City Grand Prix
It's been a stop-start journey for the Mexican City Grand Prix. Originally first held in 1962, it only ran through to 1970. It made a comeback in 1986 but again only ran through to 1992.
After a twelve-year absence, the Mexican City GP returned to the F1 calendar in 2015. Originally thought to run through just 2019, it was later announced that the race would be in place until at least 2025.
The track is unique, it's 2 km above sea level and also passes through a baseball stadium!
How long is the Mexican City Grand Prix?
The lap at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez is fairly short, just 4.3 km (2.6 miles), meaning we get a total of 71 laps during the GP.
