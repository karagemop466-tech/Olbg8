Rugby League Tips by Competition
As well as Super League, our Rugby League tipsters provide tips and predictions on the NRL, the RFL Championship, State of Origin and cup competitions such as the Challenge Cup.
The NRL (National Rugby League) is Australia's top-flight professional competition. Expect weekly NRL tips through its regular season and finals series, alongside our other Rugby League tips.
The RFL Championship is the second tier of English rugby league, directly below Super League. Our tipsters provide Championship tips and predictions alongside their Super League and NRL selections, and our Rugby League betting stats compare Championship results with Super League and the NRL, including how often favourites win and how many points matches produce.
State of Origin is the annual three-match series between New South Wales and Queensland, and our tipsters make State of Origin predictions each year.
The Challenge Cup is a knockout competition played across the first half of the year, with the final in late spring or early summer. Super League clubs enter at a later round than the Championship and community clubs, and our tipsters provide Challenge Cup tips throughout the tournament, so check back from the early rounds through to the final. Rugby League Betting Stats: Super League, Championship and NRL
These Rugby League betting stats compare Super League, the Championship and the NRL across the last five completed seasons, using results and historical match odds from OLBG's own database. They show how often favourites win, how much difference home advantage makes and how many points matches tend to produce, all useful context when weighing up Rugby League tips and predictions.
All figures cover regular-season matches only and use the result after 80 minutes, so golden point extra time is not counted. Matches awarded without being played are not included. The favourite is the team with the shorter pre-match odds, or the team for which no odds were offered because its chance of winning was too high. Matches with no odds available are left out of the favourite and price band figures. Past results do not guarantee future outcomes.
