How to Choose Rugby Union Tips to Back
Every Rugby Union tip on this page comes from an OLBG tipster and is recorded, so you can judge a prediction on its merits before you back it. Read the comments alongside each selection to see the reasoning behind the tip, then decide whether you agree with it.
The tips table gives you more to go on. The percentage of tips on a selection shows how much support it has among our tipsters, and the value rating compares the odds with that level of support to highlight potential value. Look out for tips from our Experts too: these are OLBG tipsters with high strike rates, or strong current-month or six-month profit, in Rugby Union.

Building a Rugby Union Accumulator from Our Tips
An accumulator (or acca) combines several selections into one bet, but every leg has to win for it to pay out, so OLBG's tipsters typically back win singles instead.
If you would still like a fun bet, you could build a Rugby Union acca from several teams to win in the tips on this page.
To make this easy, click
