When will you have ice hockey betting tips?
The National Hockey League runs from late September through to June, so during this period you'll find plenty of NHL betting tips on this page.
The British Elite League and the Swedish Hockey League both run from September to April.
Between them, these leagues mean there are ice hockey betting predictions to be found across most of the year.

When NHL Betting Peaks
Tip volume builds through the regular season and reaches its peak in the spring, when the Stanley Cup playoffs get under way. Sixteen teams — eight from each conference — compete across four best-of-seven rounds, so a single playoff run can stretch to well over twenty games.
With every series a best-of-seven and games coming thick and fast, the playoffs are the busiest time on this page for NHL predictions, right through to the Stanley Cup Final. It's also when you'll find the most outright betting tips, as tipsters weigh up who goes all the way.
Free NHL Money Line Predictions
This market is a simple bet on whether the designated home or away team will win. In the NHL, the money line is always two-way — overtime and a shootout are included. For European leagues, check whether your bookmaker offers a two-way (including OT) or three-way (regulation only) market, as this affects how the bet settles.
What you'll see are the odds for either team. For example, the home team may be 4/7 to win, meaning if you staked £10 and it was successful, your return would be £15.71 — a profit of £5.71.
If instead you fancy the away team, you would back them: say their money line odds were 6/4 and you staked £10, you could win as much as £25 (£15 profit) if successful.
You might want to add NHL money line picks to an accumulator. This builds up the odds for a bigger potential return, but every selection has to win for that return to pay out.
NHL Accumulator Example
As an example, imagine backing four home favourites to win on the same night:
Selection 1 — 4/7 (1.57)
Selection 2 — 4/6 (1.67)
Selection 3 — 8/13 (1.62)
Selection 4 — 2/5 (1.40)
Converting the fractions to decimal odds is the easiest way to estimate a return: multiply the odds together (1.57 × 1.67 × 1.62 × 1.40), which here gives 5.95.
That means the accumulator, if all four selections win, would return roughly £59.50 from a £10 stake — a profit of £49.50.
NHL Puck Line Predictions
As well as the money line market, you'll find tips on two other markets here for today's NHL predictions. The first is spread betting, better known as the Puck Line in the NHL.
It works the same way as Asian handicap betting in football or the run line in baseball: the favourite is given a handicap to overcome, while the underdog gets a head start to defend.
In the NHL, this line is usually set at 1.5, so the favourite is given a handicap of -1.5 and the underdog gets +1.5. It can occasionally differ — 0.5 or 2.5, for instance — depending on how strong the favourite is.
To settle the bet: if the team with the -1.5 handicap wins by two or more goals, that line wins; otherwise, the +1.5 line wins.
NHL Totals Predictions
