Most Popular F1 & Motor Racing Tips
The tips table at the top of this page lists upcoming races and season-long markets, ordered by the number of tips on each event's most popular selection. Beside each event, you will see that selection, along with the number and percentage of tips it has received.
Value Rating for Motor Racing Tips
The Value Rating shown beside the most popular tips compares the odds on offer for a selection with its theoretical chance of winning, based on the percentage of tips it has received. The higher the rating, the more value a selection may offer compared with its odds, up to a maximum of five stars. It is a guide to value rather than a guarantee of a winner.
How Can I See More Detail on F1 & Motor Racing Tips?
Click any event name in the tips table at the top of this page to open the full event page, which shows every market tipped for that event.
For each outcome, you will see how many OLBG tipsters have tipped it compared with the total number of tips on that market, giving you the percentage of support for that outcome.
At the top of each event page are two boxes. The Most Popular Pick is the outcome that has received the most tips for that event. The Best Tipster's Pick is the tip from the tipster with the highest profit who has tipped on the event.
Next to each tip, you can also see the tipster's annual profit and strike rate, their recent form and the reasoning in their comment, so you can judge the tip before deciding whether to back it.
How to Back F1 & Motor Racing Tips
Once you have found a tip you like, use the "Add" button to add it to your betslip. You can do this from this page or from any event page.
When your betslip is complete, you can compare the odds offered by different bookmakers. Choose a bookmaker and your betslip is transferred to their site, where you can place your bet.

F1 Betting Tips: Qualifying, Sprint & Race Predictions
OLBG tipsters cover the whole F1 season, from the opening round to the final Grand Prix. Every race weekend has F1 tips and predictions for pole position and the race, with the same markets tipped for the sprint on sprint weekends, plus ante-post markets throughout the season.
Market
What the tip covers
Fastest Qualifier
Pole position: the driver who sets the fastest qualifying time (each-way available)
Win Race
Race winner: the driver who wins the race or sprint (each-way available)
Podium Finish
A driver to finish in the top three
Top 6 Finish
A driver to finish in the top six
Points Finish
A driver to finish in a points-scoring position
Fastest Lap
The driver who sets the quickest lap
Win Tournament
Ante-post winner markets, such as the Drivers' Championship, including "Without" versions with a named favourite removed
Each-way terms vary between bookmakers, with two or three places typically paid, so check the terms before you place a bet. Tips usually appear two to three days before the race weekend. For the latest news and information on upcoming races, visit the
official F1 website
.
