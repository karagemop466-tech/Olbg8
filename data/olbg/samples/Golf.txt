Golf Betting Markets We Cover
Under the "Tournaments" tab, you will find predictions for a range of golf betting markets, including:
Tournament Outright Winner
This prediction is for a golfer to win the tournament. It can be a "Win" bet, where the golfer has to win the event, or an "Each Way" selection, where the golfer usually needs to finish in the top five to eight places for a partial payout. Place terms vary from tournament to tournament and are often a quarter or a fifth of the odds for the place part of the bet. Bookmakers may offer up to eight or even ten places for big tournaments such as the Masters and the US Open. Place terms are displayed alongside the odds before you place a bet, so check you are happy with them before you finalise any wager.
First Round Leader
This is similar to the outright winner market, but the tip is for the golfer leading after round one. Selections can again be placed to "Win" or "Each Way". Each way terms usually cover only the first few places, and the number of places varies by bookmaker, so check before placing a bet.
Top American, Top European and Top GB & Ireland
These markets focus on golfers from a single region. You are still betting on which golfer finishes in the best position, but they only need to beat the other players in that market, not win the tournament. Each way betting is usually available in these markets for the main events, but not always for regular weekly tournaments, depending on how many players from the region are in the field.
Team Event Markets
For team events such as the Ryder Cup and Presidents Cup, we also cover three markets:
Overall correct score:
the final points score between the two teams.
Top combined points scorer:
the player who scores the most points across both teams.
Top team points scorer:
the player who scores the most points for their own team.
Betting Odds and Tip Confidence
The selection odds are shown alongside the number of tips received compared with the total number of tips on all outcomes in the market, which gives the confidence percentage. Click the "tips and comments" tab in the navigation menu to sort the predictions by volume, in ascending or descending order.
Value Rating
The Value Rating column shows a rating of one to five stars, with five stars indicating the strongest value. It compares the percentage of tips a selection has received with the chance of winning implied by its odds. If the tip percentage is higher than the chance implied by the odds, that is a sign the selection could be a value bet. Click the Value Rating column to sort selections by number of stars.
