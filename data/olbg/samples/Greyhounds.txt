How Our Greyhound Racing Tips Work
Each day, this page shows the most popular free greyhound racing tips and predictions from OLBG members for today's UK meetings.
Every race listed above shows the most-tipped dog, its current odds, how many win tips it has received and its share of the tips in that race. The star value rating compares the dog's odds with its chance of winning as implied by that share of the tips, so the more stars, the more generous the price looks against the weight of tipping support.

How greyhound races are graded
Graded races make up most of the daily cards. Each race carries a letter for its distance category and a number for its class, with 1 the highest grade (so A1 is stronger than A5). The most common prefixes you'll see on a racecard are:
Prefix
Race type
A
Standard-distance graded race
D
Sprint
S
Stayers
E
Marathon
P
Puppy race
OR
Open race, where dogs are selected on merit rather than by grade
Traps, reserves and jacket colours
British greyhound races have a maximum of six runners, one from each trap. If a dog is withdrawn through injury or illness, a reserve can take its place when a suitable one is available. Reserves are marked with an ‘R’ on the racecard and on their racing jacket.
Each trap has its own jacket colour:
Trap 1: Red
Trap 2: Blue
Trap 3: White
Trap 4: Black
Trap 5: Orange
Trap 6: Black and white stripes

Greyhound Derby
The English Greyhound Derby is the sport's flagship event, run over 500m with heats through May and the final in early June. Wimbledon hosted it from 1985 to 2016, before the track closed the following year. Since then it has been staged at Towcester, apart from 2019 and 2020 when Nottingham took over.
