Tennis Tips and Predictions for ATP, WTA and Grand Slam Tournaments
OLBG's tennis tipsters share free tennis tips and predictions for ATP Tour and WTA Tour events, including the four Grand Slams. Tips are also posted for lower-tier events such as the ATP Challenger Tour and WTA 125 tournaments, and for team competitions like the United Cup, Davis Cup and Billie Jean King Cup.
The Grand Slams and the weekly ATP and WTA Tour events usually attract the most tips, as they are widely shown on TV and streaming services. That means you can find tennis predictions for most of the big tournaments throughout the season.

How tennis picks are listed
This page lists the most popular free tennis picks on OLBG today. Selections can cover matches and tournaments over the next week or two, so click the 'Events' column heading to sort the tennis predictions by date. Where a match is listed, the selection is for a betting market on that match. Where a tournament is listed, for example 'US Open' or 'US Open Women', the selection is for the tournament winner market. To check the upcoming schedule, see the official
ATP Tour website
for the men's events and the official
WTA Tour website
for the women's events.
Tennis betting markets we show tips for
Tournament Winner
The most popular pick under a tournament heading is the selection with the most tips to win the tournament outright. Tour events are listed by name, for example 'ATP Rome' or 'WTA Madrid'. Tournament Winner picks can also include each-way selections. Each-way terms vary by bookmaker, but they commonly pay on a player reaching the final, so check the terms before you bet. Click the most popular pick to open the tournament tips page, which shows every selection placed, including the each-way picks.
Win Match
This is a straight bet on a player to win the match, with no other conditions. The most popular pick shown for each match is the player most tipsters have selected to win. Rules on retirements and walkovers vary between bookmakers, so check your bookmaker's tennis betting rules before you place your bet.
