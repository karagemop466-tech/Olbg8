Gaelic Football Competitions to Bet On
Gaelic Football tips cover a wide range of competitions across the season, from the national leagues right through to the club game. Here are the main ones you can expect selections for:
National Football League:
The league is the main early-season competition, running through the spring. It is split into four divisions, with Division 1 featuring the top counties and Divisions 2 to 4 below it. Teams are promoted and relegated between divisions each year, and the divisional structure means there is competitive football and plenty of tipping opportunities across all four tiers.
All-Ireland Senior Football Championship (Sam Maguire):
The showpiece of the Gaelic Football calendar. The winners lift the Sam Maguire Cup, and this is the competition every county wants to win. It runs through the summer and culminates in the All-Ireland final at Croke Park.
Tailteann Cup:
The secondary inter-county championship, contested by teams outside the top tier of the Sam Maguire competition. It has quickly become an important part of the season, giving more counties a realistic shot at silverware and adding depth to the summer's tipping.
Provincial Championships:
Before the All-Ireland series, counties compete in their provincial championships — Munster, Leinster, Ulster and Connacht. These are steeped in tradition and local rivalry, and they feed into the wider All-Ireland picture.
Sigerson Cup:
The leading third-level (university and college) competition, often a first look at the emerging talent who go on to star at inter-county level.
Club Football:
Club championships run at county, provincial and All-Ireland level, with the season traditionally reaching its climax on St Patrick's Day. Club football offers a huge volume of matches to get involved in.
Ladies' Gaelic Football:
We also cover the Ladies' game, including the league and the All-Ireland Ladies' Senior Football Championship.
All-Ireland Football Championship Winners
The main tournament is the
All-Ireland Football Championship
, established in 1887. This is a long-standing tournament, which is an inter-county competition.
Kerry
hold the all-time record with 39 titles, ahead of
Dublin
on 31 — the only two counties to have reached double figures.
The reigning champions are
Mayo
, who ended a 75-year wait in 2026, defeating Kerry in the final to claim their fourth title.
