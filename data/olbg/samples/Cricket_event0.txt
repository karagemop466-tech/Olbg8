Summary of Tipster comments
India is heavily favoured to win the third T20 match against New Zealand, leading the series 2-0. The Indian batting lineup, including Rohit Sharma, Virat Kohli, and Hardik Pandya, has shown strong performance, while New Zealand's batting, particularly Kane Williamson, has been inconsistent.
