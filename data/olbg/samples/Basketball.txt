Main basketball betting markets
The three main markets, which are similar across American sports, are Money Line, Pointspread and Game Totals. All three are covered by our free basketball betting tips and predictions.
Market
What you are betting on
How it works
Money Line
The winner of the game
A straight win bet on the home or away team. Tips on a team to win the game are Money Line predictions.
Pointspread
The winner after a points handicap is applied
Similar to an Asian handicap. A team at -3 must win by at least 4 points to win the bet, while the opposing team at +3 wins the bet unless it loses by 4 or more. A 3-point win is a push, which returns your stake.
Game Totals
The combined points scored by both teams
An over or under bet on a set line. For example, with a line of 216.5, the over needs at least 217 points in total to win.
Overtime usually counts towards settlement for all three markets, but always check your bookmaker's basketball settlement rules before placing a bet.
Basketball betting advice
As with any sport, it helps to start with leagues you know and where plenty of information is available, such as the NBA. Team stats, recent form and injury news are a good starting point for building confidence in a selection, and you should only stake what you can afford to lose.

How our free basketball tips and event pages work
The tips feed at the top of this page lists free basketball betting tips and predictions for upcoming games, covering individual matches as well as outright markets such as the NBA Championship winner.
What each tip in the feed shows
For each event, the feed highlights the most tipped outcome and the market it comes from: Money Line, Pointspread or Game Total. Alongside it, you can see how many tipsters have backed that outcome out of the total number of tips on the market, the best available odds and the option to add the selection to your betslip.
What you can see on an event page
Click an event name to open its event page. This shows the 'Most Popular Tip', the selection with the most predictions, and the 'Best Tipsters Tip', which comes from the tipster with the highest 12-month profit for basketball tips. You can then view every outcome in each of the three markets, with the number of tipsters behind each one.
