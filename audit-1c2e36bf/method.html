METHOD / PROVENANCE

A gate, not a guarantee.

How the historical ledger was assessed, what the checks actually do, and what they cannot prove.

Important: This is a standalone review of commit 43c3c96d52b0f80bfc8c7e7eba114406805d9d57. It does not modify the root desk, automatically verify the web, predict results, or publish recommendations. Sources and markets can change after the stated review time.

What was reviewed

The snapshot contains 53 historical published records. We read the original pick payload and the originator code, then manually checked the specific findings and event-only crosswalks linked in the review. The compact snapshot retains a SHA-256 of the original input. We did not manually establish every factual statement in all 53 records. Unreviewed facts are not labelled verified.

Four additional findings are scoped separately to PR #37 at commit 19b53ff54743e03f8eaf25a299ab1bd162a4cefe, reviewed at 06:42 UTC; they must not be read as assertions about later revisions.

The table identifies a source in one of two ways: event identity only means the linked official page was read for that pairing or event; reference only is a relevant sporting-body directory and does not establish the event. Either status says nothing by itself about injury, a live betting offer, or probability accuracy.

Sources include Cricket Ireland's ODI report, the NHL's score feed, the WTA Tokyo draw, and Lega Volley’s fixture preview.

What the optional gate checks

  1. Fixture and time: a named event, an explicitly zoned start instant in the future, and an event-specific, reviewed source with an excerpt.
  2. Claim-level support: separately identified official evidence for form, match history, statistics and material availability. A generic sports homepage cannot substitute for an event-specific excerpt.
  3. Offer and settlement: an exact market and selection, source-linked American odds and timestamp, pre-event availability, settlement period, and refund/draw rules. The default maximum quote age is four hours, a conservative operational setting rather than an official source or a promise that the market remains open.
  4. Independent pricing: named official inputs, a stated method independent of tipster-vote share, and a recorded, temporally held-out evaluation with an artifact, sample size and Brier score. These are minimum documentation requirements, not proof that the evaluation is valid.
  5. Written analysis: at least 30 words, a clearly named selection, no exact duplicate prose after whitespace and case normalization within the evaluated set, and neither of the two prohibited words in the prediction.

All checks are fail closed: missing or malformed input becomes a reason to withhold. They operate on recorded metadata and do not fetch sites. A human reviewer or a trustworthy source adapter still has to read and confirm the actual source. A structural pass is called reviewed_schema_only, not a prediction or an assurance.

Odds and pushes

For a one-unit stake with win probability p_win, push probability p_push, and decimal odds d, expected net profit is p_win × (d − 1) − (1 − p_win − p_push). At a fair price this equals zero, so fair decimal odds are (1 − p_push) / p_win. A push returns the stake; it is not a sporting draw and must not be counted as a win or loss.

The gate applies the −250 to +10000 restriction to the recorded offered American price, not merely a separately derived fair line. It accepts valid American odds at the endpoints but rejects invalid values such as −99, 0 or +99. These arithmetic checks do not certify the offer remains available.

Reproducing the review

python3 -m unittest discover -s audit-1c2e36bf -p 'test_*.py' -v
python3 audit-1c2e36bf/build_report.py --check

The tests use explicitly synthetic candidates and do not create betting advice. To regenerate the historical compact snapshot, run extract.py on the exact original pick JSON: it checks the SHA-256 and refuses changed input. See the README for the schema and limitations.
