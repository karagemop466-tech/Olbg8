3. Data irregularities spotted on OLBG

  • Handball selection labels. Eleven of fifteen handball rows showed the most-tipped selection as literally Tie. A draw being the single most-tipped outcome in eleven matches is implausible, so we treat the label as a display fault and price the match ourselves rather than reading it as a drawn match. Handball page.
  • Conflicting most-tipped selections. On the main ice hockey page, Ambri Piotta v EHC Biel showed a puck line (-1.50) as the most-tipped market; the Swiss league sub-page showed the plain money line for the same fixture. EHC Kloten v HC Ajoie showed Kloten -1.50 on one page and HC Ajoie on the other. Swiss National League page.
  • Relative time labels. OLBG prints Today and Tomorrow without a date or a timezone. We kept the wording exactly as displayed and did not convert it to a clock time.
  • Format mismatch. OLBG lists South Africa v Australia and Bangladesh v West Indies without a format; the ICC fixtures page shows both as Tests. We follow the ICC.
